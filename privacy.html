Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 14, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how CrossyBot ("we", "our", or "us") collects, uses, and protects user data when connecting Telegram with supported social media platforms (including TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, and others) via their official APIs.

2. Information We Access

We follow strict data minimization principles and only request permissions required to publish content on your behalf:

  • OAuth Access & Refresh Tokens: Granted securely via official platform authorization flows (TikTok OAuth, Google/YouTube OAuth, Meta/Instagram Graph API) to authenticate media uploads without accessing your passwords.
  • Basic Account Identifiers: Platform-specific user IDs (such as OpenID or Channel/Account ID) and display names used solely to identify connected accounts within the Telegram bot interface.
  • Media & Metadata: Video files, titles, captions, and privacy settings submitted by you in Telegram for cross-platform distribution.

3. Data Storage & Retention

We do not sell, rent, or store personal user profiles or media archives on external databases:

  • Temporary Media Processing: Video files sent to the bot are cached temporarily during API transmission and deleted immediately once publishing completes or fails.
  • Token Security: OAuth tokens are stored locally in restricted environment configurations strictly to maintain authorized API sessions.

4. Third-Party API Services

CrossyBot interacts directly with official third-party APIs. Your data transmitted through these integrations is also subject to each provider's respective privacy policy:

  • TikTok Open API: Governed by the TikTok Privacy Policy.
  • YouTube API Services: Governed by the Google Privacy Policy.
  • Instagram Graph API: Governed by the Meta Privacy Policy.
  • Telegram Bot API: Governed by the Telegram Privacy Policy.

5. Data Revocation & Deletion

You retain complete control over your connected accounts and may revoke access at any time:

  • TikTok: Settings and privacy → Security & permissions → Manage app permissions.
  • YouTube / Google: Google Account Security Settings → Third-party apps with account access.
  • Instagram: Settings → Apps and websites → Active apps and websites.

Once access is revoked on any platform, all associated tokens become invalid and cannot be used further.

6. Children's Privacy

CrossyBot is not intended for use by individuals under the age of 13 (or the minimum legal age required in your jurisdiction), and we do not knowingly collect data from minors.

7. Contact

For questions, security concerns, or data deletion inquiries, please open an issue in the official GitHub repository for this project.
